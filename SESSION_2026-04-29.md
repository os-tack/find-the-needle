## What's actually broken (per →1681 audit)

`src/commands/bench.rs:2070` polls journal.jsonl for these events:
```
"event":"agent.(completed|died_early|panic|stalled)"
```
But the agent loop emits **`api.error`** on credit / auth / rate-limit failures and exits without writing any of the four "terminal" events. bench polls until `wall_clock_s + 60s` (≥660s), SIGTERMs, reports `0 turns / 0 tokens / stop=deadline_exceeded`.

That's why every credit-capped cell took 11 minutes instead of 5 seconds. With ~80% of cells hitting the cap, a 1-hour sweep stretched to 6+ hours.

The misleading `[kernel] spawn returned: attach: ostk attach bench` line I was treating as a "bootstrap race" is actually a **success message** from `ostk kernel spawn` (it's saying "spawned, here's how to attach"). My readiness probe at `bench.rs:1481-1494` was solving a non-existent problem. My classify_failure rule treating it as transient was retrying credit-cap failures 3x.
